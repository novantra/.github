## What Novantra does

Configure any process. Capture evidence. Prove compliance against any framework. Cloud or on-premise.

Novantra runs configurable lifecycles for compliance, HR, onboarding, due diligence, regulator submissions, case management, and fulfilment, with public-access participants and audit-grade evidence.

## What you can do with it

- **Design any operation.** Configurable lifecycles, no-code, audit-grade. From audit programmes to onboarding lifecycles to regulator submissions, you configure the process once and run it across the organization.
- **Capture every participant.** Public-access fulfilment lets external parties (suppliers, applicants, beneficiaries) complete their part without an account, with the same audit chain as internal work.
- **Sovereign by architecture.** Database-per-organization isolation. Customer-managed encryption keys through KMS or HSM. Customer-owned object storage. Cloud or on-premise. Multi-region availability across MENA and Switzerland.

## Designed for the frameworks you operate under

International and European frameworks (GDPR, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, NIS2, FINMA), plus the MENA frameworks (ADHICS, SAMA, NCA). You register the frameworks you use, with the requirements and coverage that matter to you.
